## ** Zones & Site Planning **

- ** Zone 0 ** – The home or central hub.Example: a house icon.
- ** Zone 1 ** – Intensive use(kitchen garden, herbs).Example: vegetable patch symbol.
- ** Zone 2 ** – Orchards, perennials, compost bins.Example: orchard trees grouped.
- ** Zone 3 ** – Fields, grazing.Example: crop rows.
- ** Zone 4 ** – Semi - wild(firewood, timber).Example: forest edge.
- ** Zone 5 ** – Untouched wild zone.Example: pine trees / wilderness icon.

** Illustrations : using free draw line/curve/circle + drag(zone 1 - 5) **

## Color Coding Scheme

The ** most effective zoning colors ** are intuitive — warmer colors near the home(activity center), cooler / natural tones as you move outward.

| Zone         | Suggested Color                    | Symbolism                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ** Zone 0 ** | 🟧 ** Bright Orange or Red **      | Energy, activity, human presence  |
| ** Zone 1 ** | 🟨 ** Golden Yellow **             | Productivity, warmth, high use    |
| ** Zone 2 ** | 🟩 ** Light Green **               | Growth, garden area, daily care   |
| ** Zone 3 ** | 🟩** Medium / Darker Green **      | Fields, orchards, stable crops    |
| ** Zone 4 ** | 🟫 ** Brown / Olive Green **       | Woodland, forage, less managed    |
| ** Zone 5 ** | 🟦 ** Blue - Green / Deep Green ** | Natural ecosystem, untouched land |
